Look inside the decision.

Signal Lab turns messages into categories, scores and review suggestions. You define the questions. Jev evaluates each message. Your routing rules decide what happens next.

The demo contains 128 synthetic messages and saved, real Jev responses. These are model assessments, not ground-truth labels or measured sales outcomes.

Switching views and changing routing thresholds use saved results. Editing the actual questions marks results as pending until you analyze again.

Settings are saved on this browser. Imported messages and live results stay in memory until you export them. This workbench suggests routes; it does not send replies or modify your inbox.
