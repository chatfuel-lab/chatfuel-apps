## The salon's day, under your own name

A nail or lash studio's Instagram fills with the same two questions under every post — how much, and when. This app is the business behind those questions: a booking calendar customers reach through the channels you connect, a client list, an inbox, a knowledge base that holds your real prices, and a Comment Studio that answers price-and-time comments with a fixed public reply and a Direct quoting those prices — in Spanish, Portuguese or English.

## Set up in five steps

Business, specialities, services, hours, team. Services come pre-filled per speciality with prices anchored to your country — edit, delete, publish. Everything lands in Bookings and the Knowledge Base at once, and running set-up again updates what changed instead of duplicating it.

## Team and money, without a second tool

Owner, manager and specialist levels with e-mail invitations; a specialist opens the app and works in her own column. Money shows what the salon earned by service, specialist and client for the period, next to expenses and supplies, with a CSV export — computed from the bookings themselves, no double entry.

## Yours to change

The wizard scaffolds the full source into your repository: four plain React modules under src/modules, two SQL migrations for your Supabase project, a mail sender for staff invitations, and a build plan your coding agent follows step by step. The presets, the texts, the levels — all of it is yours to change.
